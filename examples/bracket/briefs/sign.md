# Projecting sign bracket

A café wants a bracket for a sign that projects from its outside wall. The sign
hangs from the bracket's tip, at least 250 mm clear of the wall. With wind and
snow allowance, design for 120 N at the tip. The café is on the coast, so the
bracket must be stainless steel.

- A safety factor of at least 3.
- The tip deflects at most 2 mm, so the sign does not visibly sag.
- The upright leg is at most 150 mm tall, to fit between the window head and the
  gutter.
