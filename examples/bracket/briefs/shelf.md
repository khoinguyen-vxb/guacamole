# Garage shelf bracket

We need wall brackets for a garage shelf. Each bracket carries up to 250 N at the
front edge of a 180 mm deep shelf; design for that load at the bracket's tip. The
garage is damp, so the brackets should be aluminium.

- A safety factor of at least 2 at the design load.
- The shelf tip deflects no more than 3 mm under that load.
- Each bracket, with its screws and washers, weighs no more than 0.5 kg.
- No wider than 60 mm along the wall, and reaching at least 180 mm out from it.
- Fixed to the masonry wall with M6 screws.
