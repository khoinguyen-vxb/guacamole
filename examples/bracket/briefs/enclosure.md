# Enclosure support bracket

A 5 kg electronics enclosure, 120 mm deep, mounts on a factory wall on a pair of
brackets. Each bracket must carry 150 N at its tip, which covers half the weight
under a 2 g shock with margin. Use painted structural steel S275.

- A safety factor of at least 2.5 at the design load.
- Each bracket assembly weighs at most 0.35 kg, fasteners included.
- At most 50 mm wide, and at least 120 mm deep so the enclosure sits fully on it.
