## 4. Consequences for the actual-solid trade

These are proposed analysis practices within the validated task, not new requirements or selected design values:

- Compare spine counts 1–4 and both tripod/quadpod CFRP leg arrangements. Include all required mounting, support and retention parts before comparing assembled mass; tube-only mass cannot decide the structure.
- Retain 8/6 and larger 14/12 roll-wrapped CFRP as distinct section alternatives. Treat the 6/5 tube as a thin-wall sensitivity case with unidentified construction, not as mechanically interchangeable stock.
- The aluminium comparison applies to spines; it does not replace the required CFRP legs.
- Use explicit assumed CFRP density/stiffness ranges until candidate-specific data are obtained. Record those ranges as hypotheses, not vendor-certified bounds. PETG grade, printed density, infill/void treatment and anisotropy also remain unestablished.
- Preserve nominal tube dimensions separately from fabrication tolerances and clearance allowances. A general ±0.1 mm catalogue statement is not a complete fit specification or a guarantee of worst-case bore/OD combinations.
- Model sleeve, insert, adhesive, clamp, fastener and anti-rotation load paths. Splayed-leg ground reactions are not necessarily axial. Nominally separated rails do not establish assembly bending or torsional stiffness without shear-transfer evidence.
- Prepare whole-stock cutting schedules with explicit kerf and end trimming. Share compatible stock between spines and legs only when the exact material/section matches; count each purchased tube once. Include adhesive and fastener minimum packs separately. PETG printed-part cost is exempt, but its installed mass is not.
- Keep commercial mass overrides separate from envelope-solid density mass. A complete installed ledger must also include propulsion, battery, electronics, actuators, bearings, wiring, connectors and retention without double counting supplier-included accessories.
- Neither this register nor the completed synthetic FreeCAD capability probe establishes vehicle geometry, complete mass, strength, cost compliance or landing clearance. Those require the saved candidate assemblies and artifact-bound checks.
